Salamander

Key commitment in AEAD · invisible salamanders

Forge one real AES-GCM ciphertext and tag that WebCrypto accepts under two different keys, decrypting to two different messages — then watch the security verdict fire even though every tag check passes.

WHY IT MATTERS

Every system that reads “the tag verified” as “this is the message that was sent to me” is trusting a property AES-GCM never promised. It cost Facebook Messenger a blind spot in its abuse-reporting pipeline.
